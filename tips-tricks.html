03 — SURVIVAL GUIDE

TIPS + TRICKS

Things you figure out after making the mistake once.

01
Learn Your Route
Walk your class route before you actually need it. Five extra minutes of exploring beats being five minutes late.
02
Save Your Syllabus
Keep your syllabi somewhere easy to find. Put important deadlines into your calendar before the semester gets chaotic.
03
Carry the Essentials
Phone, charger, water, Bearcat Card, headphones and something to eat. Your backpack is basically your mobile survival kit.
04
Don't Skip Everything
You do not have to attend every event. But try something new occasionally. Clubs and campus events are an easy way to meet people.
05
Ask For Help
Professors, advisors, classmates, resident advisors and campus staff are there for a reason. Asking early is usually easier than fixing it later.
06
Leave Room For Chaos
Your perfectly planned day will probably not stay perfect. A missed shuttle, unexpected rain or wrong building happens. Adapt and keep moving.
