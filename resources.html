UC Housing

UC Academic Support

Academic Writing Center (AWC) Free one-on-one writing help for all UC students and subjects. Available in person or online through Zoom. You can work with a tutor on papers and assignments. You can also submit an essay for written feedback. Feedback is typically returned within 48 hours. Online feedback accepts essays up to 6 double-spaced pages or 3 single-spaced pages. Peer Tutoring One-on-one tutoring for many undergraduate courses. Available in person or online. Tutors specialize in specific subjects and can help explain course material and answer questions. Supplemental Review Sessions (SRS / MATH 1096) A 1-credit course designed to support certain MATH/STAT courses. Students work in small groups to review and practice material from their companion course. Math and Science Support (MASS) Center Offers tutor-led group study tables. Supports many math- and science-related courses, including calculus and chemistry. Academic Coaching One-on-one support for improving college success and study skills. Available online or in person. Useful for things like organization, time management, studying, and adjusting to college. Success Skills Workshops Group workshops focused on improving college success skills. Led by tutors and offered online.

UC Mental Health Support

Counseling and Psychological Services (CAPS) Provides mental health support to UC students. Counselors use a collaborative, strengths-based approach to help students identify tools, resources, and strategies. Support can range from one meeting to ongoing assistance, depending on what the student needs. Rapid Access Counseling (RAC) Students can schedule a Rapid Access Counseling appointment online. You can also schedule by phone during business hours: Monday–Friday, 8:30 AM–5:00 PM 513-556-0648 Crisis Support Provides immediate support for students experiencing an acute mental health crisis. Available after hours and during crises by calling: 513-556-0648 Follow the prompts to be connected with care. For Family or UC Community Members Parents, family members, or other UC community members who are concerned about a student can call 513-556-0648 and request to speak with a CAPS counselor for consultation.

Maps are an important part of Bearcat: No Tutorial, helping players learn how to navigate the University of Cincinnati campus while experiencing realistic freshman challenges. The game uses campus maps to represent routes between dorms, classrooms, dining halls, libraries, and other key locations, making navigation an interactive part of the gameplay.

UC Get Involved

GetInvolvedUC is UC's online hub for discovering student organizations, campus events, and ways to get involved outside the classroom. Students can browse organizations by interests, find events, and request to join organizations directly through the platform. It can help you: Find student organizations — academic, professional, cultural, recreational, creative, and more. Discover campus events — including social, educational, and community events. Meet people and build community through clubs and activities. Develop leadership skills through organizations and leadership opportunities. Find volunteer and community-engagement opportunities. Use CORQ, UC's companion mobile app, to discover events and groups and manage event passes. UC's Center for Student Involvement specifically recommends using GetInvolvedUC to search for organizations based on your interests and find a community on campus

Career Studio

You don't have to wait until graduation to start thinking about what comes next. Career Studio can help students with things like resumes, interviews, networking, career exploration, internships, and co-ops. Even if you have absolutely no idea what you want to do yet, it can be useful for figuring out what opportunities are available and how to prepare for them.
