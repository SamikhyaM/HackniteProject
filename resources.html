02 — KNOW YOUR CAMPUS

UC
RESOURCES

Starting college means suddenly having access to a ridiculous number of websites, portals, systems, and tools. These are some of the ones you're most likely to actually use.

01

Bearcat Portal

The Bearcat Portal is one of the main places to start when you need to find UC student information and services. It brings together access to important student tools and resources, so you don't have to remember where every individual system lives. As a new student, it is worth getting familiar with the portal early because you'll probably return to it throughout your time at UC.

VISIT BEARCAT PORTAL →
02

Catalyst

Catalyst is UC's student information system. This is where you'll deal with many of the official parts of being a student, including course registration, your class schedule, academic information, financial information, and your student record. If you're trying to figure out something official about your classes or student account, Catalyst is one of the first places you should check.

VISIT CATALYST →
03

Canopy + Canvas

Your classes don't stop existing when you leave the classroom. Canvas is where you'll often find course materials, assignments, announcements, grades, and other information from your professors. Checking your courses regularly is one of those boring habits that can save you from realizing at 11:47 PM that something was due at midnight.

VISIT CANOPY →
04

IT Help

Technology is going to be a huge part of your life at UC, which also means something will eventually stop working when you need it most. UC's Digital Technology Solutions provides support for technology-related problems, accounts, passwords, and other digital services. Save this website before you actually have a problem with something.

VISIT IT HELP →
05

UC Calendar

There is almost always something happening around campus, but finding it can be another story. The UC Calendar collects university events, activities, workshops, important dates, and other campus happenings. If you're sitting in your dorm wondering what everyone else is doing, this is a good place to start.

VISIT UC CALENDAR →
06

Career Studio

You don't have to wait until graduation to start thinking about what comes next. Career Studio can help students with things like resumes, interviews, networking, career exploration, internships, and co-ops. Even if you have absolutely no idea what you want to do yet, it can be useful for figuring out what opportunities are available and how to prepare for them.

VISIT CAREER STUDIO →
