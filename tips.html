03 — SURVIVAL GUIDE

TIPS +
TRICKS

Nobody really gives you a tutorial for college. These are the things you figure out after making the mistake once.

01

Learn Your Route

The first time you walk to a new classroom should not be five minutes before class starts. Campus buildings can look confusing, entrances aren't always where you expect them to be, and your map doesn't always help as much as you think it will. Walk your route beforehand and give yourself extra time during your first few days.

FIVE EXTRA MINUTES OF EXPLORING BEATS BEING FIVE MINUTES LATE.
02

Save Your Syllabus

Your syllabus contains a ridiculous amount of information that becomes important later. Keep each one somewhere you can easily find it and pay attention to assignment deadlines, exam dates, grading policies, office hours, and course requirements. Putting important dates into your calendar at the beginning of the semester can save you a lot of stress later.

IF IT HAS A DEADLINE, PUT IT IN YOUR CALENDAR.
03

Build Your Backpack

Your backpack becomes your portable survival kit. Keep the things you actually need with you: your phone, charger, Bearcat Card, water, headphones, something to write with, and a snack. You don't need to carry your entire room around campus, but having the basics can save you when your schedule suddenly changes.

PHONE. CHARGER. BEARCAT CARD. WATER. SNACK. YOU'RE GOOD.
04

Try Something New

You don't need to attend every campus event or join twenty clubs. But try something that you wouldn't normally do. Go to an involvement fair, attend a club meeting, sit with someone new at lunch, or show up to an event because you saw a random poster for it. A lot of friendships start with two people deciding to go somewhere neither of them really knows anything about.

YOU DON'T HAVE TO KNOW ANYONE BEFORE YOU SHOW UP.
05

Ask For Help

Being new means you won't know everything. That's normal. If you don't understand an assignment, can't find a building, don't know where to get something done, or simply have no idea what you're supposed to be doing, ask someone. Professors, advisors, classmates, RAs, student organizations, and university staff exist for exactly these situations.

ASKING EARLY IS USUALLY EASIER THAN FIXING IT LATER.
06

Leave Room For Chaos

Something will go wrong. You will probably get lost at least once. You might miss a shuttle, forget something in your room, walk into the wrong building, get caught in the rain, or realize you have absolutely no idea where your next class is. Don't let one bad moment convince you that the entire day is ruined. College is mostly figuring things out as you go.

WRONG BUILDING? OKAY. RESTART. KEEP MOVING.
07

Don't Spend Everything

It's surprisingly easy to spend money during your first few weeks. Coffee here, food there, random Target trip, something you suddenly decide your dorm absolutely needs. Give yourself a weekly budget and keep track of what you're spending. Having money left later is much better than realizing you spent your entire budget on snacks and decorations.

YOUR FUTURE SELF WILL APPRECIATE THE MONEY YOU DIDN'T SPEND.
08

Take Care Of Yourself

College can make it very easy to prioritize everything except yourself. Eat actual meals, drink water, get enough sleep, take breaks, and give yourself time to reset. You don't need to be productive every second of the day. Sometimes the smartest thing you can do is go back to your room, close your laptop, and rest.

SURVIVING COLLEGE ALSO MEANS KNOWING WHEN TO SLOW DOWN.
