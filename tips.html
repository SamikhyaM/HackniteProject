03 — SURVIVAL GUIDE

TIPS + TRICKS

Things you figure out after making the mistake once.

01
Learn Your Route
Walk your class route before you actually need it. Five extra minutes of exploring beats being five minutes late.
02
Save Your Syllabus
Keep your syllabi somewhere easy to access and put important deadlines into your calendar before they sneak up on you.
03
Carry The Essentials
Phone, charger, water, Bearcat Card, headphones, and a snack. Your backpack is basically a mobile survival kit.
04
Don't Skip Everything
You don't have to attend every event, but try something new. Clubs and campus events are one of the easiest ways to meet people.
05
Ask For Help
Professors, advisors, classmates, RAs, and staff are there to help. Asking early is usually easier than fixing everything later.
06
Leave Room For Chaos
Missed shuttle. Random rain. Wrong building. It happens. Adapt, keep moving, and don't let one bad moment ruin your day.
