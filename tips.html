03 — SURVIVAL GUIDE

TIPS +
TRICKS

Things you'll probably figure out after making the mistake once.

01

Learn Your Route

Walk your route to class before you actually need it. Campus buildings can be confusing when you're seeing them for the first time, and your map won't always tell you which entrance or hallway you need. Give yourself a few extra minutes during your first couple of weeks so getting lost doesn't turn into being late.

02

Save Your Syllabus

Your syllabus is basically the instruction manual for each class. Keep it somewhere easy to access and pay attention to deadlines, exam dates, grading policies, office hours, and course requirements. Put important dates into your calendar early instead of trying to remember everything yourself.

03

Build Your Backpack

Your backpack becomes your portable survival kit. Keep the basics with you: your phone, charger, Bearcat Card, water, headphones, something to write with, and a snack. You don't need to carry everything you own, but having the essentials can save you when your schedule suddenly changes.

04

Try Something New

You don't need to join twenty clubs or attend every event on campus. Just try something you normally wouldn't. Go to an involvement fair, attend a club meeting, sit with someone new at lunch, or show up to an event because it looked interesting. You might meet people simply because you decided to show up.

05

Ask For Help

Being new means you won't know everything, and that's completely normal. If you don't understand an assignment, can't find a building, don't know where to get something done, or simply have no idea what you're supposed to be doing, ask someone. Professors, advisors, classmates, RAs, and university staff are all there to help.

06

Leave Room For Chaos

Something will go wrong. You might get lost, miss a shuttle, forget something in your room, walk into the wrong building, or get caught in the rain. Don't let one bad moment ruin the entire day. College is a lot of figuring things out as you go, and eventually the things that felt confusing become normal.

07

Watch Your Spending

It's surprisingly easy to spend money during your first few weeks. Coffee here, food there, a random Target trip, and suddenly your budget is disappearing. Keep track of what you're spending and give yourself a reasonable weekly limit. You don't need to avoid having fun — just make sure you still have money later.

08

Take A Break

College can make it feel like you should always be doing something. You don't. Eat properly, get enough sleep, take breaks, and give yourself time to reset. Sometimes going back to your room, putting your phone down, and doing absolutely nothing for a while is exactly what you need.
