02 — KNOW YOUR CAMPUS

UC RESOURCES

The websites that make freshman life a little less confusing.

01
Bearcat Portal
Think of this as one of your main starting points as a UC student. The Bearcat Portal brings together important student tools and information in one place. Use it to find things like academic information, student services, and other resources you'll need throughout your time at UC.
VISIT WEBSITE →
02
Catalyst
Catalyst is where a lot of the serious student-administration stuff happens. You'll use it for things such as course registration, schedules, academic information, financial details, and accessing your student record. If you're dealing with classes or your official student information, Catalyst is one of the places to check.
VISIT WEBSITE →
03
Canopy + Canvas
This is where much of your actual classwork lives online. Depending on your course, you may use Canvas to find assignments, announcements, course materials, grades, and other information from your professors. Check your courses regularly so you don't discover an assignment exists after it was due.
VISIT WEBSITE →
04
IT Help
Something isn't working? Your account, password, computer, Wi-Fi, or another piece of technology is causing problems? UC's Digital Technology Solutions provides technology support and information for students. Save this one before you actually need it — because something eventually will stop working at the worst possible time.
VISIT WEBSITE →
05
UC Calendar
UC has events happening constantly, but you won't know about all of them unless you look. The university calendar can help you find campus events, activities, workshops, important dates, and other things happening around UC. It is also a good place to look when you have absolutely no idea what to do with your free time.
VISIT WEBSITE →
06
Career Studio
You don't have to wait until senior year to think about your career. Career Studio can help with resumes, interviews, networking, career exploration, internships, co-ops, and preparing for opportunities. For a freshman, it can simply be a place to start figuring out what you might want to do next.
