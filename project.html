04 — THE WHY

WHY
DID WE
MAKE
THIS?

Because starting college feels a lot like playing a game you were never given the instructions for.

As first-year students, we realized that there are hundreds of things you are expected to figure out on your own. Where to go. What to do. Who to ask. How to get involved. How to simply survive the first few weeks.

We wanted to turn that experience into something that was actually fun. Instead of giving freshmen another checklist or information page, we built an interactive game where you can make choices, get lost, meet people, make mistakes, and figure things out.

And because everyone's first week is different, the game doesn't have to play out the same way twice.
